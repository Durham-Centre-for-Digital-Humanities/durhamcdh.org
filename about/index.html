About Us

The Durham Centre for Digital Humanities supports interdisciplinary research, collaboration, and training across humanities scholarship, digital methods, and public engagement.

About DCDH

The Durham Centre for Digital Humanities brings together humanities scholars, technical specialists, and research partners to develop ambitious digital projects, methods, and resources.

We support research across editing, archives, spatial humanities, cultural heritage, data visualisation, and computational analysis, while also creating space for training, collaboration, and public-facing work.

The centre acts as a shared point of connection across Durham University, linking departments, collections, and partner institutions through a common interest in digital humanities research.
