Durham University Alumni in the Great War

A Memorial Database — Durham University War Memorial Register, Palace Green Library

About this project

This database records Durham University alumni who died during the First World War, 1914–1918. Records are drawn from the Durham University War Memorial Register (Palace Green Library, Special Collections), verified against the Commonwealth War Graves Commission Debt of Honour Register and the Soldiers Died in the Great War dataset. The full database contains 147 records; this archived version displays a sample of 50.
