Durham Miners’ Gala: Programme Archive, 1871–1939

A catalogue of Durham Miners’ Gala programmes recording collieries, bands, banner appearances, and speakers, 1871–1939.

Michelle Dobson · MA Digital HumanitiesCompleted 2022Supervised by Prof. Claire Warwick
AHRC

About this project

The Durham Miners’ Gala has been held annually since 1871 and is one of the largest trade union gatherings in the world. This project catalogued the Gala programmes held at Durham County Record Office and Durham Mining Museum, recording participating collieries, lodge banners, bands, and platform speakers for each year from 1871 to 1939.

Data & Methodology

Gala programmes were digitised under Creative Commons licence by Durham County Record Office. Programme content was transcribed and structured into a relational database. Speaker biographies were cross-referenced against the Dictionary of Labour Biography. The website was built using Vue 3 with a Node.js/Express backend.

Data

68 annual programmes; approx. 3,400 band/banner appearances; 214 named speakers.
