Durham Assizes Criminal Register, 1800–1850

A database of criminal trials at the Durham Assizes recording defendants, offences, verdicts, and sentences, 1800–1850.

Rachel Morrow · MA HistoryCompleted 2023Supervised by Prof. Claire Warwick
MDS

About this project

This project transcribed and structured entries from the Durham Assize records held at The National Archives and Durham County Record Office, creating a searchable database of 1,847 criminal cases tried at Durham between 1800 and 1850. The database enables analysis of patterns in prosecution, verdict, and sentencing by offence type, defendant age and sex, and decade.

Data & Methodology

Assize records were identified in the ASSI series at The National Archives and in the quarter sessions rolls at DCRO. Entries were coded against a controlled vocabulary for offence types, verdicts, and sentences, informed by the Old Bailey Proceedings Online coding scheme. The website was built with Svelte and a SQLite backend.
