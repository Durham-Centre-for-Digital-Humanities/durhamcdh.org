Printed Books in Durham Cathedral Library, 1485–1640

A catalogue of pre-1640 printed books in Durham Cathedral Library, with provenance notes and ESTC concordance.

Oliver Tate · MA Book History & Digital HumanitiesCompleted 2025Supervised by Dr Richard Pears, Prof. Claire Warwick
EU ERC

About this project

Durham Cathedral Library holds one of the finest collections of early printed books in northern England. This project catalogued all pre-1640 imprints, cross-referencing against the English Short Title Catalogue and Short-Title Catalogue Netherlands, and recording provenance evidence including ownership inscriptions, bookplates, and binding evidence.

Data & Methodology

Items were identified from the Cathedral Library manuscript shelflist (DC/CD/L) and the 1960s–80s typescript handlists. Each item was physically examined and described against RBMS rare books cataloguing standards. ESTC and STCN numbers were assigned and provenance evidence transcribed verbatim. The database was built in Airtable and exported to JSON for this archive.
