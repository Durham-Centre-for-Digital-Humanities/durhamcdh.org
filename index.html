Durham CentreforDigitalHumanities

The Durham Centre for Digital Humanities stimulates, strengthens, and supports interdisciplinary digital humanities research and training at Durham University and beyond.

Where the humanities meet advanced digital research

DCDH promotes interdisciplinary research across digital scholarship, visual and material culture, archives, data, mapping, and computational methods. We create space for connection, collaboration, and training for scholars at every stage.
