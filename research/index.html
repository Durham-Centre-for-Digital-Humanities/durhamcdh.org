Research

The Durham Centre for Digital Humanities supports research across a wide range of themes, methods, and collaborative projects. Our work brings digital techniques into conversation with humanities questions, collections, and public engagement.

Research areas

Our research spans multiple methods and areas of expertise, creating opportunities for interdisciplinary collaboration across Durham University and beyond.

Digital Editing and Scholarly Publishing

We create digital editions and research resources that connect texts, metadata, images, and contextual scholarship.

Find out more

Spatial Humanities and Mapping

We use mapping, GIS, and spatial interpretation to study movement, place, and historical change.

Find out more

Text Mining and Corpus Analysis

We apply computational analysis to literary, historical, and documentary corpora across a range of scales.

Find out more

Digital Archives and Preservation

Our work supports digitisation, metadata, discovery, and long-term stewardship of humanities collections.

Find out more

3D Visualisation and Cultural Heritage

We develop digital approaches to visual culture, built heritage, objects, and immersive reconstruction.

Find out more

Data and Network Analysis

We use visualisation and network methods to understand complex relationships in historical and cultural data.

Find out more

Current and recent work

The centre supports a growing portfolio of collaborative projects across editing, archives, mapping, visual culture, and computational analysis.
